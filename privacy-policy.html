Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This privacy charter describes our zero-upload policy regarding your files and inputs processed on AllInOneTool.

1. Zero Upload / Client-Side Processing

Unlike other online utilities, all operations performed by tools on AllInOneTool are processed locally inside your browser cache. This includes image compressions, text edits, hash creations, calculations, and PDF adjustments. Your files, documents, and inputs are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in a database. They stay safely on your local device.

2. Cookies and Tracking

We do not run tracking cookies or log user data. We use standard browser local storage solely to support recently viewed tools history list which resides exclusively on your device.

3. Third-Party Ads

We may show Google AdSense advertisements. Google may use DART cookies to serve ads based on visits. You can opt out of this by reviewing the Google Ad Content network privacy policies.
